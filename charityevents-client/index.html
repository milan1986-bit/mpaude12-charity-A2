About Us

Our Mission

Hope & Harmony Foundation exists to raise funds and awareness for local families facing hardship, and to support young people through mentoring, education and recreational programs. Every event on this site is hosted by us or one of our trusted partner charities, with all proceeds going directly towards the causes described on each event page.
